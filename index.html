The work · off court

Things I’ve built.

I studied CS at Trinity College (class of 2026). I love building full-stack apps, data pipelines and machine-learning models, mostly around the two things I live with: the tour and the markets.

Featured · built for the tour

Athlete Tournament Tracker

Every touring pro asks it before booking a flight: will this tournament make or lose money? So I built a tool that tells you.

  • Models prize money across worst, realistic and best-case runs, with currency conversion, subsidies, sponsorships and spending caps.
  • Projects a full P&L before anything is booked, with year-to-date stats and runway on a dashboard.
  • A weekly scraper pulls about 600 squash tournaments from the PSA API, so details fill themselves in.
  • Google sign-in, a versioned OpenAPI 3.1 contract checked in CI, and mobile-first layouts.

Rylo

110 commits · MIT
Stock sentiment platform for retail traders

Pulls StockTwits and Reddit signals and classifies them with FinBERT. Includes a six-signal stock screener, sector rotation across 15 sectors, and “Wong”, an AI analyst that answers ticker questions using RAG.

r-c-model

413 commits
ML model for short-term stock direction

Outputs calibrated probabilities of up, neutral or down over 3, 5 and 20-day horizons. Features cover momentum, volatility, macro, options and sentiment. GitHub Actions runs predictions twice a day and retrains the model every week.

LEAP Screener

32 commits
A daily options screener with a gated pipeline

Picks pass a macro “kill switch”, then fundamentals, multi-timeframe technicals, options liquidity and IV, then a sentiment check. It runs on a schedule and backtests sectors over 10 years using Black-Scholes.
