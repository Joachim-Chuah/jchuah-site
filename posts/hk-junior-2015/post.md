---
title: Hong Kong Junior Open, U11
date: 2015 · Hong Kong
---
I’d only been playing squash for about two years when I went to Hong Kong for the Junior Open.

Honestly, the trophy isn’t what I remember most. I was just incredibly happy to be travelling somewhere new, and to be getting there because of squash.

Winning the Under-11 title was a bonus. Looking back, it was the first of a lot of places this sport would take me.
